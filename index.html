Guardrails included.

Define your floor price, discount limits, quantity breaks and negotiation rules once. Parley's merchant agent enforces them on every single offer, automatically -- it handles the conversation, the rules stay yours.

A separate, deterministic guardrail layer re-checks every AI proposal before it ever reaches a buyer, so the negotiation strategy can be flexible while the business terms never are.
